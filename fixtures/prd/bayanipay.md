# BayaniPay merchant launch

Fictional product. BayaniPay is not a real company. This PRD exists so a staged mock call can disagree with it.

## 1. Product

BayaniPay is a peso wallet for families receiving money from overseas. The merchant console shows a processing fee and a payout status. This document is the scope contract for the launch build.

## 2. Scope by phase

### 2.1 Phase 1 — Onboarding

Phase 1 is account signup, identity check (KYC), and the peso wallet. Card charging is out of Phase 1.

### 2.2 Phase 2 — Payment integration

Payment integration ships in Phase 2. It is not part of Phase 1. Phase 2 covers card checkout, InstaPay cash-in, and merchant payment links.

## 3. Pricing

The processing fee on the merchant dashboard is 2.4 percent. Changing that number needs finance sign-off before anyone tells a client it moved.

## 4. Out of scope

Discounts, multi-currency wallets, and white-label bank partners are not in this launch.
